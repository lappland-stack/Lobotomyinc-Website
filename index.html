Kenopsia and its ties to grief, and liminality(WIP)

SO TO PREFACE ALL THIS, this is an informal paper, there are some book names and statistics used, but i didnt faact check them much, and i wont either, but you are not getting a deep and fulfilling and full of data and research sort of article, youre getting someones feelings and educated opinion on this, but mostly feelings, kenopsia is a feeling after all, so its only fitting its explained by someone pouring their own feelings into this

So kenopsia, weird word, its literal meaning, from "The Dictionary of Obscure Sorrows", is in short The Eeriness of Places Left behind and is described as follows: "You can sense it when you move out of a house - noticing just how empty a place can feel.Walking through a school hallway in the evening, an unlit office on a weekend, or playgrounds out of season. They're usually bustling with life, but now, lie abandoned adn quiet." Now, as i am 19 at the time of writing this, i finished highschool at 16, so 3~ years ago, and i feel like i did not feel this when i finished because of the speed at which modern life moves at, but in writing this and other late walks i realised that this is just what I told myself, in reality i felt it, it was the final day, we already were out of school for maybe 3 weeks at this point since all teaching stopped, and it was just exams now, and we had this big assembly, with gongratulations and final goodbyes and tears, and see i walked around a bit, then went out the door for one last time, and i think the worst part is that, i only spent 1 year at this school, i never truky got to have my highschool years in one spot tbh, first year was in london, second, third and fourth were in wellingborough, 1 of which was during covid which gave me an even higher apathetic disconnect from it all, and the last year was in northampton where i spent about 5 months barely talking to anyone, family included, but by the end i was doing better emotionally and mentally, and yet, having only spent 1 year, hell even less considering a school year does not go for a full 12 months, i still felt odd, like going back to my home country, and seeing the places i used to play at as a kid, changed, and with no kids, matter of fact i have a whole trip planned, where i go to all the places i used to live, and just wander, nothing more, maybe interact with 1-2 people, but just wander, and i hypothesise that the feeling of kenopsia will be the most prevalent, as i personally see that a part of this feeling isnt just the empty places, but seeing the fact that they changed in your absence and are just as empty as the time you las set foot out of them. Another big idea i have is to take advantage of the photography section of ,my website and go to abandoned places, especially malls since as a 2006 kid malls are a big part of me growing up, and post covid and also moving away from eastern europe to far west past the long forgotten iron curtain, a lot of these were left behind, my current town has a pretty big mall, and only a single section is still mostly alive, the rest ? Closed down stores with empty promises and many that dont even hold their original logos and brand banners, just the local council posters and banners, and see, anyone who might come here now wont ever know of the old section of the mall where the local warhammer shop was, and the first time, my now best friend, introduced me to warhammer and showed me some of the minifigures. That was 2~ years ago, the store is long closed and everything to do with it is long long gone, just the room itself basically, and i only just started my first few warhammer minifigs now, i need to paint 5 more and i have some decent basic units, but i digress, quite heavily so, the point of all that yapping was to not only better explain the feeling of kenopsia itself to myself and the unfortunate person who stumbeled upon this website and its contents, but also to possibly jog your memories a bit too, yes you, the reader, a paper like this can surely be kept professional and to the point, but i hold the opinion that feelings have to be conveyed in a way that actually connects with a reader, moreover there is no corporate board or some fuckass algorithm i need to follow, so this gives me the power to do as i see fit. Now, as much as kenopsia, by its meaning as seen in The Dictionary of Obscure Sorrows (hereby referd to as TDOS, i aint writing all that every time my guy), and my stupid little storytelling, this feeling is mostly used to describe empty places, eg the house right before moving, and the school after hours or on the final day, but, unless theres another feeling in TDOS that covers this, the ffeeling of kenopsia does not mention the people themselves outside of just a general note, by that i mean, kenopsia does mention the lack of people yet it does not bring it to a personal feeling, in school you didnt really know or liked everyone did you ? No shame, me neither twin, but the lack of all the people you personally knew and tied to the place, make their absence, further emphasise the feeling as a whole, and i believe this could be tied to the feeling of emptiness following the loss of someone. This is where it gets a bit more serious, since death and other such topics will be discussed, the top was lighthearted for a reason, wether i fully intended it or not tbh. But the feeling after a loved one is gone, wether death, and its many causes, or they left your life, broke up, fell out of friendship, divorece, sure there may be anger, resentment, especially if the person is still alive, but even then there will be a point where our inherent humanity will make us look at those places and remember the people. If the person is alive you might call them and tell them to "come get their shit", but if they are dead, there wont be a person to answer that call, the phone may still be available, and charged, on a nightstand with an alarm someone else snoozed because its original target is now gone, but the person ? No longer there. So, kenopsia, although a non clinical term and as part of a book not recognised as part of any diagnosing or treatment procedure, i believe that the overall meaning and its ties to feelings of emptiness or places that are empty without the peopel that made them alive, can be tied to depression, specifically depression post loss, yes grief falls here too, but grief can and will go away, but unchecked anf poorly managed or understood grief can fall into depression, and i believe taking this into consideration can help with dealing with grief and depression following grief, now i will say this, although late, i am not a psycologist or a professional in this field, im just a young adult who is going through some shit, and this helps me and i hope having this here may one day help another person, hence why i kept it without references, or any heavy clinical terminology. But now that we got over what kenopsia is and what i believe its ties to grief and depression are, i think its time for liminality, so that deserves its own extended section. It originates from the latin word limen, which means threshold, and that is because liminality is meant to signify a point of transition, a point stuck in time, like the bit of kenopsia that talks about the school (yes if it isnt yet obvious schools are a very big and general way to explain all this i dont have that many options on hand please) where a BIG chapter in your life ends, and you sort of just walk out like any other last day of school before summer, however, you dont often go back now do you ? You will reminisce about those days, however distant your age may make them, and yet, if you ever go back, or even seeing the images themselves, makes them feel..... off. Your friends from the 4th seat in the 3rd column on the right side of the class are most likely no longer in your life, and if they are you reached the stage of "hows the wife and kids" and not "hey so can you ask your mom if its okay for me to come over", you passed that threshold, yet part of you will forever remain there, and kenopsia greatly ties to it, as it inherently means the absence of people from places that didnt move on. Now to bring it all together, kenopsia ties to giref, depression (that has as a root cause grief), and liminality, by its inhernet need of transitional phase and areas and the loss or lack off people, especially those close to the individual.
